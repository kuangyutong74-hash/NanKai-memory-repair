Six project-bound landmark sprites were generated separately with the built-in image generation tool, each using the corresponding user-supplied real photograph as its sole architectural reference: Nankai campus bell; Siyuan Hall; Patriotic Three Questions memorial monument; historic Mu Zhai Library; Nankai main building with Zhou Enlai statue; modern Jinnan campus library. Shared generation direction: transform only the referenced real landmark into a faithful high-detail 32-bit pixel-art cutout; preserve its silhouette, proportions, materials, facade rhythm and distinctive structural features; consistent three-quarter isometric view; warm late-afternoon light from upper left; subtle deep-purple shadows; crisp pixel clusters; clean grounded base with a small patch of paving or greenery; genuine transparent alpha; center the complete landmark with generous padding; no UI, frame, watermark or unrelated buildings. Landmark-specific prompts additionally preserved the photographed bell support, Siyuan Hall columns, Three Questions monument geometry, Mu Zhai Library dome, main-building tower and Zhou Enlai statue, and Jinnan Library's red-brick fins and broad white roof slab.
